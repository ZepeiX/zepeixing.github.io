Zepei Xing

Postdoctoral researcher CIERA, Northwestern University.

7203

1800 Sherman Ave

Evanston, Chicago, Illinois

I am a theoretical astrophysicist studying stellar and binary evolution, with a particular interest in the physics of mass transfer.

My work explores a range of binary populations, including X-ray binaries, gravitational-wave sources, subdwarf+Be binaries, and Wolf-Rayet+O binaries. I was a core developer of the population synthesis code POSYDON and I use MESA to investigate how mass transfer shapes the evolution of binary systems.
